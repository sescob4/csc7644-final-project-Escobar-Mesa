New York First-Time Visitor Guide

Overview
New York City is known for iconic landmarks, shopping, rooftop bars, museums, restaurants, nightlife, Broadway, and diverse neighborhoods. Manhattan is often the main focus for first-time visitors because many major attractions are located there.

New York Geography

Manhattan
Manhattan is an island bordered by the Hudson River and the East River.

Highlights:
- Connected to Brooklyn, Queens, and the Bronx by bridges.
- Approximately 13.4 miles long.
- Approximately 2.3 miles wide.
- Dense concentration of attractions and neighborhoods.

Travel tip from the source:
- Planning activities by area helps reduce unnecessary travel across the city.

Getting Around New York

Subway
The New York City subway system is one of the fastest ways to travel around Manhattan.

Highlights:
- MetroCards available at subway stations.
- Express trains skip stops for faster travel.
- Local trains stop at every station.

Travel tip from the source:
- Check whether a train is express or local before boarding to avoid missing your stop.

Yellow Cabs
Yellow taxis are widely available throughout Manhattan.

Highlights:
- Easy to flag down.
- Convenient for shorter trips.
- Iconic New York transportation experience.

New York Pass

Overview
The New York Pass provides admission to many major attractions and allows visitors to skip some ticket lines.

Included attractions mentioned in the source:
- Top of the Rock.
- 9/11 Memorial Museum.
- New York Water Taxi Tour.

Travel tip from the source:
- A shorter-duration pass may be more useful than purchasing one for the entire trip.

Top Attractions for First-Time Visitors

Top of the Rock
Location:
- Rockefeller Center, Midtown Manhattan

Top of the Rock offers panoramic views of New York City and a direct view of the Empire State Building.

Highlights:
- 360-degree skyline views.
- Empire State Building views.
- Observation deck experience.

Travel note from the source:
- Admission is timed and queues may occur during busy periods.

National September 11 Memorial and Museum
Location:
- Lower Manhattan

The 9/11 Memorial and Museum honors the victims and history of the September 11 attacks.

Highlights:
- Reflecting memorial pools.
- Underground museum exhibits.
- Historical artifacts.
- Emotional and educational experience.

Statue of Liberty and Water Taxi Tours
Location:
- Battery Park, Lower Manhattan

Boat tours from Battery Park provide views of the Statue of Liberty, the Brooklyn Bridge, and the Manhattan skyline.

Highlights:
- Hudson River views.
- Statue of Liberty sightseeing.
- Brooklyn Bridge views.
- Skyline photography.

Travel notes from the source:
- Round-trip water taxi tours last approximately 1 hour and 30 minutes.
- Statue Cruises is required for visitors wanting to climb the Statue of Liberty or visit Ellis Island.

Travel tip from the source:
- Sunset and dusk rides provide especially strong skyline views.

The High Line

Overview
The High Line is an elevated park built on a former railroad line along Manhattan’s west side.

Highlights:
- Elevated walking paths.
- West Manhattan views.
- Pedestrian-only experience.
- Access to the Meatpacking District.

Travel note from the source:
- Access to the High Line is free.

Tipping Etiquette in New York

Overview
Tipping is considered an important part of hospitality culture in New York and the United States.

Guidelines from the source:
- Restaurant tips generally range from 15% to 22%.
- Taxi drivers are commonly tipped.
- Hotel staff and bellhops are commonly tipped.

Travel tip from the source:
- Carry small bills for tipping.
