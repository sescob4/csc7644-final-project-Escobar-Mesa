Atlanta Travel Guide: 30 Reasons To Explore Atlanta

Overview
Atlanta is preparing to welcome visitors from around the world during the FIFA World Cup. The city combines major attractions, civil rights history, museums, sports, nightlife, parks, street art, breweries, food halls, entertainment districts, and cultural landmarks across Downtown, Midtown, Buckhead, the BeltLine, Decatur, Summerhill, and surrounding metro areas.
