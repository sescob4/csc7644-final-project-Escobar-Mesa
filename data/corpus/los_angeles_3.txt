Los Angeles Food Guide: Must-Try Foods and Restaurants

Overview
Los Angeles is known for its diverse food culture, including authentic Mexican food, Korean barbecue, creative cafes, Asian fusion restaurants, bakeries, and dessert spots. Many neighborhoods have local favorites and late-night food options that reflect the city’s multicultural food scene.

Authentic Mexican Food

Tacos Los Cholos
Tacos Los Cholos is a Southern California taqueria chain known for grilled meats and tacos with options ranging from chicken to filet mignon. The restaurant is often busy but lines move quickly. Customers can customize tacos at a self-serve salsa and toppings bar.

Popular items include:
- Street tacos with chopped grilled meat, onions, cilantro, and salsa on corn tortillas.
- AHM Taco, which includes a flour tortilla stuffed with meat and a cheesy Anaheim grilled pepper.
- Rice and beans as side dishes.

Highlights:
- Affordable tacos.
- Late-night food option.
- Popular self-serve salsa bar.
- Known for grilled meats.

Recommended rating:
- 9.5/10

Avenue 26 Tacos
Avenue 26 Tacos is a popular late-night taco truck in Los Angeles known for affordable tacos and a busy street-food atmosphere. Customers customize tacos at a self-serve salsa bar with onions, cilantro, pickles, and sauces.

Popular items include:
- Chicken tacos.
- Pork tacos.
- Beef tacos.
- Spicy green sauce.
- Potatoes soaked in taco meat juices.

Highlights:
- Late-night dining.
- Street food atmosphere.
- Affordable tacos.
- Popular local taco spot.

Recommended rating:

Porto’s Bakery
Porto’s Bakery is a famous family-owned Cuban bakery founded in 1976. It is known for pastries, cakes, Cuban snacks, and desserts. Many visitors consider it one of the most iconic bakeries in Los Angeles.

Popular items include:
- Potato balls (papa rellena).
- Cheese rolls.
- Cuban-style corn tamales.
- Spanish-style caramel flan.
- Strawberry Mojito drink.
- Guava lemonade.

Highlights:
- Cuban bakery specialties.
- Affordable pastries and snacks.
- Popular Los Angeles food destination.
- Good stop for desserts and lunch.

Recommended rating:
- 10/10

Los Angeles Food Planning Tips
Los Angeles food culture is spread across many neighborhoods, so it is best to group restaurants by area.

Good food-focused combinations include:
- Koreatown: Park’s BBQ.
- Chinatown: Steep LA.
- Pasadena: Republik Coffee, Woon, Mandarin Coffee Stand, Amigo’s Restaurant and Cantina.
- Late-night food: Avenue 26 Tacos and Tacos Los Cholos.
- Bakery and dessert stops: Porto’s Bakery and 626 Ice Cream.

For late-night food, consider Avenue 26 Tacos or Tacos Los Cholos.

For coffee and cafes, consider Philz Coffee, Republik Coffee, or Mandarin Coffee Stand.

For Asian-inspired dining, consider Park’s BBQ, Woon, Steep LA, or Ototo.

For desserts and pastries, consider Porto’s Bakery or 626 Ice Cream.
