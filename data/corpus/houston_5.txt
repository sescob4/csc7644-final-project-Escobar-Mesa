Houston Foodie Guide

Overview
Houston is known for its diverse restaurant scene, including Tex-Mex, steaks, burgers, seafood, Japanese food, Thai food, Middle Eastern cuisine, and international dining experiences. Many restaurants are located around the Galleria area, Midtown, and Kemah Boardwalk.

Mexican Restaurants in Houston

Overview
Houston has a large number of Mexican and Tex-Mex restaurants run by Mexican chefs and families. Margaritas are especially popular throughout Texas.

Highlights:
- Frozen margaritas.
- Margaritas on the rocks.
- Flavored margaritas.
- Tex-Mex cuisine.

Steakhouses in Houston

Tango Malbec
Tango Malbec is an Argentinian restaurant in the Galleria area known for steaks and Argentinian cuisine.

Highlights:
- Argentinian steaks.
- Upscale atmosphere.
- Galleria location.

Pricing information from the source:
- Steaks range from approximately $35 to $70.
- Steaks are served separately from side dishes.

Papa Bros Steakhouse
Papa Bros Steakhouse is a traditional steakhouse with multiple steak options.

Highlights:
- Large steak selection.
- Classic steakhouse atmosphere.

Houston’s Restaurant
Houston’s Restaurant is known for steaks and other American dishes.

Highlights:
- Steaks.
- Popular local recommendation.
- Frequently busy dining location.

Burger Restaurants in Houston

Bubbas Burger Shack
Bubbas Burger Shack is a roadside burger restaurant known for large burgers and buffalo burgers.

Recommended items:
- Buffalo Vegas Burger.
- Monterey Jack cheese.
- Bacon.
- Avocado.
- Egg.

Highlights:
- Casual roadside burger spot.
- Buffalo burgers.
- Texas beer pairing.

Pricing information from the source:
- Burgers start around $4.75.
- Larger burgers cost around $10 to $11.

Recommended drink:
- Lone Star Texas beer.

Cheesecake Factory
Cheesecake Factory is a chain restaurant known for burgers and cheesecakes.

Highlights:
- Large burger menu.
- Cheesecakes.
- Avocado and bacon burgers.
- Large dessert portions.

Travel note from the source:
- Some cheesecakes are described as extremely sweet.

Grand Lux Cafe
Grand Lux Cafe is similar to Cheesecake Factory but with a more elegant atmosphere.

Highlights:
- Burgers.
- Casual global cuisine.
- Relaxed but upscale atmosphere.

Japanese Restaurants in Houston

Gyu-Kaku Japanese BBQ
Gyu-Kaku is a Japanese barbecue restaurant in Midtown where guests grill meats and vegetables at their table.

Highlights:
- Table barbecue grills.
- Raw meats and vegetables for grilling.
- Seafood options.
- Large appetizer menu.
- Good drinks menu.

Pricing information from the source:
- The course for two costs around $60.

Travel notes from the source:
- Portions are large enough for more than two people.
- Clothes and hair may smell like barbecue afterward.

Kona Grill
Kona Grill is located near the Galleria Mall and includes sushi and seafood dishes.

Recommended items:
- Miso-sake seabass.
- Shrimp and pork fried rice.
- Pan-Asian ratatouille.

Highlights:
- Sushi bar.
- Galleria location.
- Large desserts.
- Asian-inspired dishes.

Sage 400
Sage 400 is a sushi and Japanese restaurant near the Galleria area.

Highlights:
- Sushi.
- Sashimi.
- Rolls.
- Cocktails.
- Dimly lit atmosphere.

Seafood Restaurants in Houston

Yia Yia Mary’s
Yia Yia Mary’s is a Greek seafood restaurant with Mediterranean-style decor and seafood dishes.

Recommended items:
- Seafood pot.
- Baklava dessert.

Highlights:
- Mediterranean atmosphere.
- Fresh seafood display.
- Greek cuisine.

Kemah Boardwalk Restaurants

Overview
Kemah Boardwalk contains several seafood restaurants and waterfront dining options.

Travel note from the source:
- Restaurants are often crowded and may require waiting for a table.

Bubba Gump Shrimp Co.
Bubba Gump Shrimp Co. is a seafood restaurant inspired by the Forrest Gump movie.

Highlights:
- Seafood chain restaurant.
- Forrest Gump theme.
- Waterfront dining.

Bayou City Seafood and Pasta
Bayou City Seafood and Pasta serves seafood dishes near the waterfront.

Recommended items:
- Crawfish baskets.
- Frog legs.

Fun and Unique Restaurants in Houston

Studio Movie Grill
Studio Movie Grill combines a movie theater with food and drink service during films.

Highlights:
- Food service during movies.
- Tables inside theater rooms.
- Waiter call buttons.
- Cinema dining experience.

Thai Gourmet
Thai Gourmet is a Thai restaurant known for authentic curries and spicy dishes.

Highlights:
- Thai curries.
- Authentic Thai flavors.
- Friendly owner.

Spice levels from the source:
- Spicy.
- Hot spicy.
- Thai spicy.

Travel note from the source:
- Thai spicy dishes are extremely spicy.

Little Sheep Hot Pot
Little Sheep Hot Pot is a Mongolian hot pot restaurant.

Highlights:
- Hot pot dining.
- Fresh ingredients.
- Large ingredient selection.
- Affordable prices.

Mazaj
Mazaj is a Middle Eastern hookah restaurant with a patio dining area.

Highlights:
- Hookah service.
- Middle Eastern cuisine.
- Flavorful meat dishes.
- Outdoor patio with lights and heaters.

Arcodoro
Arcodoro is a Sardinian Italian restaurant.

Highlights:
- Italian cuisine.
- Outdoor patio.
- Upscale dining atmosphere.

Travel note from the source:
- The restaurant is described as somewhat overrated despite good food.
