Atlanta Food Guide 2025

Overview
Atlanta’s food scene includes Korean barbecue, pizza, Michelin-starred tasting menus, vegan brunch, tapas, burgers, Southern cuisine, Taiwanese food, Persian food, omakase, and chef collaboration dinners. Restaurants are spread across neighborhoods including Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Poncey-Highland, Old Fourth Ward, West Midtown, and Sandy Springs.
