What to Do on Your First Visit to Los Angeles (For Families & Couples)

Visiting Los Angeles for the first time can feel overwhelming. The city is filled with “must-see” lists that make it seem possible to do everything in one trip. It’s not. But if you plan it right, your first visit to Los Angeles can be incredible. This guide covers what to do on your first trip to Los Angeles, with tips for families and couples, plus what to skip so you don’t waste time sitting in traffic.

First-Time LA Tip: Location Matters More Than Anything

Los Angeles is a collection of neighborhoods, not one central city. It is also very large, spanning about 469 square miles. Where you stay will shape your entire trip.

Best areas for first-time visitors include Santa Monica or the Westside for a beachy and walkable experience, Hollywood or West Hollywood for iconic sights and nightlife, Downtown Los Angeles for museums and food, and Studio City or the East Valley for proximity to Universal Studios and Griffith Park. Visitors should avoid planning more than two or three activities per day and keep them in the same area due to traffic.

Must-Do Experiences for First-Time Visitors to Los Angeles

Visiting the beach is essential, but not all beaches need to be seen. Santa Monica Pier offers a classic experience with attractions and is family-friendly. Manhattan Beach is cleaner and more relaxed, making it better for couples. Malibu, including Zuma Beach and Point Dume, offers scenic views and fewer crowds. Venice Beach is optional and can feel chaotic for some visitors.

Seeing Los Angeles from above is one of the best free activities. Griffith Observatory is iconic and ideal for sunset views. Griffith Park offers accessible hiking trails, and Hollywood Hills overlooks provide scenic views of the city.

Food is a major part of the Los Angeles experience. Popular choices include street tacos and visiting the Original Farmers Market at The Grove, which offers a combination of food, shopping, and atmosphere. Visitors are encouraged to choose dining locations based on proximity rather than popularity to avoid unnecessary travel time.

What to Do in Los Angeles With Kids

Family-friendly attractions include Universal Studios Hollywood, which is one of the best theme parks for first-time visitors. Santa Monica Pier combines rides, beach access, and food options. The California Science Center is free and interactive, making it ideal for children. Griffith Park provides open space and outdoor exploration, while the Natural History Museum offers extensive exhibits. Noah’s Ark at the Skirball Cultural Center provides a hands-on creative experience for younger visitors.

Families should avoid scheduling multiple major attractions in a single day and allow for downtime to prevent fatigue.

What to Do in Los Angeles for Couples

Couples can enjoy romantic and iconic experiences such as watching the sunset at Griffith Observatory, dining in Malibu with ocean views, having rooftop drinks in West Hollywood or Downtown Los Angeles, and taking evening walks in Santa Monica or along the Venice Canals.

Cultural experiences include visiting the Getty Center, which offers free admission and scenic views, and the Broad Museum, which features modern art in a downtown setting. Exploring neighborhoods such as Silver Lake, Los Feliz, and West Hollywood provides a more local experience.

Couples tend to enjoy Los Angeles more when they take a slower pace, focusing on fewer activities and more time spent enjoying each location.

What First-Time Visitors to Los Angeles Should Skip

Visitors should avoid trying to see the entire city in one trip, overbooking their schedules, and driving during rush hours, typically between 7–10 AM and 3–7 PM. It is also important not to assume that short distances mean quick travel times due to traffic conditions.

Final Advice for Your First Trip to Los Angeles

A first visit to Los Angeles should feel relaxed rather than rushed. Visitors should choose one or two main areas per day, plan a few key experiences, and leave time for enjoying the beach, scenic views, photos, and local food. This approach creates a more enjoyable and memorable experience.
