Atlanta Travel Guide

Overview
Atlanta is the capital of Georgia and one of the most influential cities in the Southern United States. The city is known for museums, historic landmarks, Southern food, civil rights history, entertainment, festivals, film locations, sports, and diverse neighborhoods.

Top Attractions in Atlanta

Centennial Olympic Park
Location:
- Downtown Atlanta

Centennial Olympic Park was built for the 1996 Summer Olympics and remains one of the city’s central gathering spaces.

Nearby attractions:
- National Center for Civil and Human Rights.
- Georgia Aquarium.
- World of Coca-Cola.
- Chick-fil-A College Football Hall of Fame.
