New Orleans Food, Attractions, and Travel Guide

Overview
New Orleans, Louisiana, is known for its food, nightlife, historic neighborhoods, live music, cocktails, streetcars, and unique cultural atmosphere. Visitors often spend time exploring the French Quarter, Garden District, City Park, and local restaurants and bars.

Best Places to Eat and Drink in New Orleans

Cafe du Monde
Cafe du Monde is one of the most famous food destinations in New Orleans and has operated since 1862. It is known for beignets and chicory coffee.

Popular items include:
- Beignets covered in powdered sugar.
- Chicory coffee.
- Cafe au lait.

Highlights:
- Historic New Orleans cafe.
- Open 24 hours a day except Christmas Day.
- Famous French-style beignets.
- Classic French Quarter experience.

Travel tips:
- Bring cash because credit cards are not accepted.
- Expect lines during busy hours.

Location:
French Quarter, New Orleans

Suggested time to visit:
- 30 minutes to 1 hour.

Muriel’s
Muriel’s is an upscale New Orleans restaurant known for attentive service and refined Southern-inspired dishes.

Popular items include:
- Duck Breast Jambalaya.
- Wood Grilled Gulf Fish.
- Vegetable Curry.
- Saints and Sinners dessert with angel food cake, peanut butter mousse, devil’s food cake, dark chocolate ganache, and dulce de leche ice cream.

Highlights:
- Fine dining atmosphere.
- Popular dinner destination.
- Well-known desserts.
- French Quarter dining experience.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 2 hours.

Brennan’s
Brennan’s is a classic New Orleans restaurant known for its pink exterior and elegant interior design. It is a famous brunch and breakfast location in the city.

Highlights:
- Historic New Orleans restaurant.
- Popular brunch location.
- Famous pink exterior.
- French Quarter dining destination.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 2 hours.

Erin Rose Bar and Killer Poboys
Erin Rose is an Irish-style pub located in the French Quarter. The bar serves local beer and also hosts Killer Poboys in the back.

Popular items include:
- Shrimp po'boy sandwiches.
- Dixie lager beer.

Highlights:
- Casual local atmosphere.
- Irish pub setting.
- Popular po'boy destination.
- Open-air French Quarter bar experience.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 2 hours.

Pat O’Brien’s
Pat O’Brien’s is one of the most famous bars in New Orleans and is best known for its Hurricane cocktail.

Popular items include:
- Hurricane cocktail.

Highlights:
- Historic New Orleans bar.
- Courtyard seating area.
- Popular nightlife destination.
- French Quarter cocktail experience.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 2 hours.

Bacchanal Wine
Bacchanal Wine is located in the Bywater neighborhood and combines a wine shop with a casual dining and outdoor social atmosphere.

Popular items include:
- Wine flights.
- Cheese boards.

Highlights:
- Local neighborhood atmosphere.
- Outdoor seating.
- Wine-focused experience.
- Popular Bywater destination.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 3 hours.

Things To Do in New Orleans

Explore the French Quarter
The French Quarter is the most famous neighborhood in New Orleans and is known for historic architecture, nightlife, restaurants, bars, balconies, music, and tourism.

Popular areas include:
- Bourbon Street.
- Frenchmen Street.

Bourbon Street is known for nightlife and tourism, while Frenchmen Street is often considered more local and music-focused.

Highlights:
- Historic New Orleans neighborhood.
- Live music and nightlife.
- Restaurants and bars.
- Walking and sightseeing destination.

Suggested time to visit:
- Several hours or multiple visits.

Ride the New Orleans Streetcar
The New Orleans streetcar system is one of the most iconic transportation experiences in the city. While slower than other transportation options, it provides a scenic way to explore neighborhoods.

Highlights:
- Historic transportation system.
- Scenic city views.
- Access to multiple neighborhoods.
- Classic New Orleans experience.

Suggested time to visit:
- 30 minutes to several hours depending on route.

Visit the Garden District
The Garden District is known for historic mansions, tree-lined streets, and beautiful residential architecture.

Popular nearby area:
- Magazine Street.

Magazine Street features cafes, restaurants, and boutique shops.

Highlights:
- Historic homes and architecture.
- Walkable neighborhood.
- Shopping and cafes.
- Scenic residential streets.

Suggested time to visit:
- 2 to 4 hours.

Volt Coffee
Volt Coffee is a cafe located near Magazine Street and is known for coffee drinks and pastries.

Highlights:
- Local coffee shop.
- Casual atmosphere.
- Good stop while exploring the Garden District.

Visit City Park
City Park is one of the largest parks in New Orleans and includes walking areas, bridges, green space, and filming locations.

Highlights:
- Outdoor recreation.
- Scenic bridges.
- Walking paths.
- Relaxing green space.

Travel tip:
- Uber may be faster than taking the streetcar depending on traffic and route timing.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 3 hours.

Go on a Ghost Tour
Ghost tours are one of the most popular nighttime activities in New Orleans due to the city’s history and reputation for ghost stories and haunted locations.

Ghost City Tours
The Killers and Thrillers tour by Ghost City Tours focuses on spooky stories, local legends, and historic crime stories around New Orleans.

Tour details:
- Approximately 1.5 to 2 hours.
- Walking tour format.

Highlights:
- Storytelling-focused experience.
- Nighttime walking activity.
- Historic and haunted New Orleans themes.

Travel tip:
- Drinks are commonly purchased before the tour.
