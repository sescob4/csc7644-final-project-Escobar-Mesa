New York Food and Restaurant Guide

Overview
New York City is known for its diverse food scene, including pizza, bagels, fine dining, natural wine bars, bakeries, Korean cuisine, seafood, tacos, burgers, brunch, and international restaurants. The city contains thousands of restaurants across Manhattan and Brooklyn.
