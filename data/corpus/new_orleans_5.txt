New Orleans Food and Restaurant Guide

Overview
New Orleans is known for Creole cuisine, Cajun food, seafood, cocktails, po’boys, brunch spots, and historic restaurants. The city combines French, Spanish, African, Italian, and Southern culinary influences.

Classic Foods To Try in New Orleans

Beignets
Beignets are fried pastries covered in powdered sugar and are one of the most iconic foods in New Orleans.

Highlights:
- French-inspired pastry.
- Often paired with cafe au lait.
- Popular breakfast and dessert item.

Popular locations:
- Cafe du Monde.
- Cafe Beignet.

Gumbo
Gumbo is the official state dish of Louisiana and is a rich stew commonly associated with Creole and Cajun cuisine.

Highlights:
- Meat and seafood stew.
- Rice-based dish.
- Influenced by African, French, and Cajun cooking traditions.

Po’boys
Po’boys are sandwiches served on French bread and are commonly filled with fried seafood or meats.

Popular fillings:
- Fried shrimp.
- Oysters.
- Catfish.
- Duck.
- Pork belly.

Muffuletta
The muffuletta is a New Orleans sandwich made with Sicilian bread, olive salad, meats, and cheese.

Highlights:
- Italian influence in New Orleans cuisine.
- Served cold or warm.
- Famous local sandwich.

Jambalaya
Jambalaya is a rice dish influenced by Spanish, African, and French cooking traditions.

Highlights:
- Rice and meat dish.
- Spiced Creole and Cajun flavors.
- One of the signature Louisiana dishes.

Seafood
New Orleans is known for:
- Oysters.
- Shrimp.
- Crab.
- Seafood gumbo.
- Fried seafood platters.

French Quarter Restaurants

Cafe du Monde
Cafe du Monde is one of the most famous food destinations in New Orleans and has operated since 1862.

Popular items:
- Beignets.
- Cafe au lait.
- Chicory coffee.

Highlights:
- Historic French Quarter cafe.
- Open 24 hours.
- Traditional New Orleans experience.

Travel tips:
- Cash only.
- Lines move quickly.

Address:
800 Decatur St, New Orleans, LA 70116

Central Grocery & Deli
Central Grocery & Deli is known for creating the original muffuletta sandwich.

Popular items:
- Muffuletta sandwich.
- Olive salad.

Highlights:
- Historic Italian grocery store.
- Famous New Orleans sandwich destination.

Address:
923 Decatur St, New Orleans, LA 70116

Copper Monkey

Popular items:

Highlights:

Address:
725 Conti St, New Orleans, LA 70130

Deanie’s Seafood
