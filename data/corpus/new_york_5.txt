New York City Travel Guide

Overview
New York City is one of the most visited cities in the world and is known for landmarks, museums, parks, Broadway, neighborhoods, food, nightlife, and cultural diversity. Manhattan is the center of many first-time visitor attractions, though the outer boroughs also contain important neighborhoods and experiences.

Top Things To Do in New York City

Central Park
Location:
- Manhattan

Central Park spans more than 40 blocks and is one of New York’s most famous public spaces.

Highlights:
- Walking paths.
- Picnic areas.
- Sheep’s Meadow.
- Free concerts during summer.
- Theater performances.
- Free guided walks on Saturdays.
- Bethesda Fountain.
- Loeb Boathouse.
- Skyline views.

Travel note from the source:
- Guided tours are available for visitors interested in learning about filming locations, statues, and sculptures.

9/11 Memorial and Museum
Location:
- Lower Manhattan

The 9/11 Memorial and Museum commemorates the September 11 attacks and the 1993 bombing.

Highlights:
- Reflecting memorial pools.
- Freedom Tower views.
- Historical exhibits.
- Artifacts from the attacks.
- Audio recordings and survivor stories.

Travel information from the source:
- Memorial admission is free.
- Museum admission is approximately $33.
- Timed tickets are recommended.

The Metropolitan Museum of Art
Location:
- Upper East Side, Manhattan

The Met is one of the largest and most famous art museums in the world.

Highlights:
- More than 490,000 works of art.
- Greek art exhibits.
- Impressionist collections.
- Armor collections.
- Costume collections.
- Ancient art.
- Contemporary art.

Travel note from the source:
- Visitors should budget at least a half-day for the museum.

Travel information from the source:
- Admission is approximately $30.
