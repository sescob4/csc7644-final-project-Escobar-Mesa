Houston Attractions, Museums, Parks, and Travel Guide

Overview
Houston is the largest city in Texas and is known for museums, parks, space exploration, shopping, cultural diversity, and food. The city combines modern development with Texan culture and includes strong art, science, and outdoor attractions.

Museum District

Overview
Houston’s Museum District contains 19 museums focused on art, history, science, and contemporary culture. The district is located near Hermann Park and is walkable.

Houston Museum of Natural Science (HMNS)
The Houston Museum of Natural Science is known for science and nature exhibits.

Highlights:
- Dinosaur hall.
- T-Rex and Triceratops fossils.
- Minerals and gemstones gallery.
- Planetarium.
- Butterfly center with free-flying butterflies.

Museum of Fine Arts, Houston (MFAH)
The Museum of Fine Arts, Houston is one of the largest art museums in the United States and contains more than 70,000 works.

Highlights:
- Works by Monet.
- Picasso.
- Van Gogh.
- Warhol.
- Latin American art.
- Asian art.
- African art.
- Cullen Sculpture Garden.

Children’s Museum Houston
Children’s Museum Houston is an interactive museum designed for children.

Highlights:
- Educational games.
- Interactive exhibits.
- Physics activities.
- Biology activities.
- Economics learning exhibits.

Holocaust Museum Houston
Holocaust Museum Houston focuses on Holocaust history, survivor stories, human rights, and social justice.

Highlights:
- Survivor testimonies.
- Historical photographs.
- Period artifacts.
- Human rights exhibitions.
- Social justice exhibits.

The Menil Collection
The Menil Collection is a private museum featuring surrealist, tribal, and contemporary art.

Highlights:
- Works by René Magritte.
- Max Ernst.
- Pablo Picasso.
- Rothko Chapel nearby.
- Minimalist spiritual and meditation space.

Contemporary Arts Museum Houston (CAMH)
The Contemporary Arts Museum Houston focuses on modern art and experimental installations.

Highlights:
- Temporary exhibitions.
- International artists.
- Local artists.
- Avant-garde art.
- Free admission.

Parks and Gardens

Hermann Park
Hermann Park is one of Houston’s largest green spaces and is located beside the Museum District.

Highlights:
- Houston Zoo.
- McGovern Centennial Gardens.
- Miller Outdoor Theatre.
- McGovern Lake pedal boats.
- Hermann Park Railroad.
- Hiking trails.
- Cycling trails.

Houston Zoo
The Houston Zoo contains more than 6,000 animals.

Highlights:
- African forest exhibits.
- Elephant habitat.
- Family-friendly attractions.

McGovern Centennial Gardens
McGovern Centennial Gardens is a landscaped botanical garden inside Hermann Park.

Highlights:
- Winding paths.
- Waterfalls.
- Panoramic hill overlooking the park.

Miller Outdoor Theatre
Miller Outdoor Theatre hosts free performances and events.

Highlights:
- Free concerts.
- Dance performances.
- Plays and live entertainment.

Buffalo Bayou Park
Buffalo Bayou Park is a large urban park with outdoor recreation opportunities.

Highlights:
- Walking paths.
- Picnic areas.
- Kayaking.
- River views.
- Nature areas within the city.

Gerald D. Hines Waterwall Park
Gerald D. Hines Waterwall Park features a large cascading fountain and is one of Houston’s most photographed locations.

Highlights:
- 20-meter cascading fountain.
- Relaxing urban park setting.
- Popular photography location.

Space Center Houston

Overview
Space Center Houston is NASA’s visitor center connected to Johnson Space Center and is one of Houston’s most famous attractions.

Highlights:
- Rocket Park.
- Saturn V rocket.
- Independence Space Shuttle replica.
- Boeing 747 shuttle carrier aircraft.
- Apollo mission exhibits.
- Mission Control Center.
- Apollo 11 moon landing control room.
- Mars Journey Exhibition.
- Interactive space simulators.
- International Space Station docking simulations.
- Astronaut presentations and talks.

Shopping and Neighborhoods

Montrose District
Montrose is known for art galleries, vintage shops, cafes, and local culture.

Highlights:
- Art galleries.
- Vintage stores.
- Trendy cafes.
- Local culinary scene.
- Artistic atmosphere.

The Galleria
The Galleria is the largest shopping center in Texas and one of the largest malls in the United States.

Highlights:
- More than 400 stores.
- Luxury brands.
- Indoor ice rink.
- More than 50 restaurants.
- Shopping and entertainment destination.

Nearby Attractions

San Jacinto Battleground State Historic Site
This historic site marks an important event in Texas independence history.

Highlights:
- San Jacinto Monument.
- Historic museum.
- USS Texas battleship.

Travel information:
- Located about 30 minutes from Houston near Trinity Bay.

Kemah Boardwalk
Kemah Boardwalk is located on Galveston Bay and offers attractions and restaurants in a waterfront setting.

Highlights:
- Waterfront promenade.
- Family-friendly attractions.
- Restaurants.
- Relaxed atmosphere.

Armand Bayou Nature Center
Armand Bayou Nature Center is a wildlife and nature reserve.

Highlights:
- Alligators.
- Birdwatching.
- Hiking.
- Marsh landscapes.
- Native Texas wildlife.

Highlights:

Held during April.

Highlights:

- Public parade.

Houston International Festival
Held during May.

Highlights:
- International music.
- Dance performances.
- Global cuisine.
- Cultural celebration.

Texas Renaissance Festival
Held during October and November.

Highlights:
- Medieval-themed festival.
- Knights and tournaments.
- Costumes.
- Live performances.
- Historical reenactments.
