New Orleans First-Time Visitor Guide

Overview
New Orleans, Louisiana, is known for live music, Creole and Cajun food, cocktails, historic neighborhoods, nightlife, streetcars, cemeteries, and jazz culture. The French Quarter is the city’s most famous area and serves as the center for many attractions, restaurants, bars, and music venues.

Where To Stay

French Quarter
The French Quarter is one of the best areas for first-time visitors because it places travelers within walking distance of major attractions, restaurants, bars, and music venues.

Highlights:
- Historic architecture.
- Walkable neighborhood.
- Central location.
- Easy nightlife access.
- Classic New Orleans atmosphere.

Travel tips:
- Open-container laws allow drinks in plastic cups while walking.
- Hotels in the French Quarter provide convenient access to nightlife and dining.

Food and Restaurants

Classic Fine Dining Restaurants
Popular traditional New Orleans restaurants include:
- Brennan’s.
- Antoine’s.
- Arnaud’s.
- Commander’s Palace.

Commander’s Palace
Commander’s Palace is known for its jazz brunch, Creole cuisine, and historic atmosphere.

Recommended items:
- Turtle soup.
- Bread pudding.
- Bloody Mary cocktails.

Travel tips:
- Reservations recommended.
- Dress code may apply.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1.5 to 3 hours.

Po'boys
Po'boys are one of the signature foods of New Orleans and are traditionally served on French bread with fried seafood or meat fillings.

Popular po'boy restaurants include:
- Domilise’s.
- Guy’s.
- Parkway Tavern.
- Killer PoBoys.
- Crabby Jack’s.

Popular fillings:
- Fried shrimp.
- Fried oysters.
- Duck.
- Pork belly.

French Quarter Restaurant Recommendations
Recommended restaurants include:
- Port of Call.
- Yo Mama’s.
- Coop’s Place.
- Desire.
- SoBou.

Popular dishes:
- Burgers.
- Jambalaya.
- Gumbo.
- Bayou appetizers.
- Etouffee.

Travel tip:
- Many tourist-focused restaurants in the French Quarter are considered low quality compared to established local favorites.

Modern and Upscale Restaurants
Recommended higher-end restaurants include:
- R’evolution.
- Herbsaint.
- Bayona.
- August.
- Coquette.

Highlights:
- Contemporary New Orleans cuisine.
- Fine dining experiences.
- Chef-driven menus.

Breakfast Recommendations

Elizabeth’s
Elizabeth’s is known for large breakfast portions and Southern comfort food.

Popular items:
- Praline bacon.
- Fried Green Tomatoes Benedict.

Camellia Grill
Camellia Grill serves classic diner-style breakfasts in a casual atmosphere.

Popular items:
- Pecan pie.
- Traditional diner breakfast dishes.

Things To Do in New Orleans

Visit a Cemetery
New Orleans cemeteries are known for their above-ground tombs and mausoleums due to the city’s high water table.

Highlights:
- Historic burial architecture.
- Above-ground tombs.
- Ghost tour history.
- Unique photography opportunities.

Travel tips:
- Cemeteries close early.
- Guided tours are recommended for some cemeteries.

Ghost Tours
Ghost tours are popular because of New Orleans’ reputation for haunted history, voodoo legends, and paranormal stories.

Highlights:
- Haunted history.
- Walking tours.
- Storytelling experiences.
- Historic neighborhoods.

Frenchmen Street
Frenchmen Street is one of the best areas in New Orleans for live music and local nightlife.

Popular venues:
- Howlin’ Wolf.
- Maple Leaf.

Popular bands:
- Hot 8 Brass Band.
- Rebirth Brass Band.

Highlights:
- Live jazz music.
- Brass bands.
- Local nightlife atmosphere.
- Art markets and local crafts.

Travel tip:
- Frenchmen Street is often considered more authentic and less tourist-heavy than Bourbon Street.

Frenchmen Art Market
The Frenchmen Art Market features:
- Local photography.
- Handmade crafts.
- Local artwork and gifts.

Bourbon Street
Bourbon Street is the most famous nightlife street in New Orleans.

Highlights:
- Bars and nightlife.
- Karaoke venues.
- Cocktails and daiquiris.
- Live music.
- Tourist entertainment.

Travel tips:
- Best experienced briefly for first-time visitors.
- Often crowded and heavily tourist-oriented.

Recommended stop:
- Cat’s Meow karaoke bar.

Streetcar and Magazine Street
The St. Charles Avenue streetcar is one of the most iconic transportation experiences in New Orleans.

Recommended route:
- Ride to Sixth Street and explore Magazine Street.

Magazine Street highlights:
- Boutiques.
- Cafes.
- Restaurants.
- Historic homes.

Nearby attractions:
- Lafayette Cemetery No. 2.

Suggested time to visit:
- 2 to 4 hours.

Cafe du Monde
Cafe du Monde is one of the most famous food destinations in New Orleans.

Popular items:
- Beignets.
- Coffee.
- Cafe au lait.

Highlights:
- Open 24 hours.
- Historic New Orleans institution.
- French Quarter staple.

Travel tips:
- Cash only.
- Lines move quickly.
- Beignets are served hot with powdered sugar.

Swamp Tours
Swamp tours outside New Orleans provide opportunities to see Louisiana wildlife and wetlands.

Wildlife commonly seen:
- Alligators.
- Birds.
- Wetland wildlife.

Highlights:
- Nature excursions.
- Wildlife photography.
- Guided boat tours.

Travel tip:
- Many swamp tours provide transportation from the city.

Cocktails and Drinks

Popular New Orleans cocktails include:
- Hurricane.
- Sazerac.

Travel notes:
- The Sazerac cocktail originated in New Orleans.
- Pat O’Brien’s is famous for Hurricanes, though opinions on the drink vary.

Local Culture and Events

Saints Football Games
During New Orleans Saints football games, much of the city participates in celebrations and team-related events.

Highlights:
- Black and gold team colors.
- City-wide fan atmosphere.
- Local sports culture.

Useful Local Pronunciations

Common New Orleans pronunciations include:
- Burgundy = "ber-GUN-dy"
- Conti = "CON-tie"
- Lagniappe = "LAN-yap"

Transportation and Getting Around

Hop-On Hop-Off Bus
Highlights:
- Guided city overview.
- Transportation between major attractions.
- Good introduction for first-time visitors.

Walking
Many major attractions in the French Quarter are walkable.

Travel tips:
- Comfortable shoes recommended.
- Walking is one of the best ways to explore the city.

Bicycle Tours
Bike tours are popular for exploring neighborhoods and learning city layout.

Highlights:
- Guided exploration.
- Neighborhood access.
- Outdoor sightseeing.

Late Night Food

Lucky Dog Vendors
Lucky Dog hot dog carts are common throughout the French Quarter and are a popular late-night food option.

Planning Recommendations

For first-time visitors:
- Stay in the French Quarter.
- Visit Bourbon Street once.
- Spend evenings on Frenchmen Street.
- Ride the St. Charles streetcar.
- Explore Magazine Street.

For food-focused trips:
- Eat beignets at Cafe du Monde.
- Try a po'boy.
- Visit Commander’s Palace.
- Explore local breakfast restaurants.

For nightlife and music:
- Frenchmen Street.
- Bourbon Street.
- Brass band performances.
- Jazz clubs and karaoke bars.

For history and culture:
- Cemetery tours.
- Ghost tours.
- Historic neighborhoods.
- Streetcar routes.

For outdoor and scenic experiences:
- Swamp tours.
- Magazine Street walks.
- Streetcar rides.
