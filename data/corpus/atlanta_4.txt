Atlanta Travel Guide: The Best of the ATL

Overview
Atlanta is known for parks, food, sports, nightlife, civil rights history, breweries, museums, shopping, and entertainment. The city combines Southern culture with modern neighborhoods, walkable trails, and major attractions spread across Midtown, Buckhead, Downtown, Inman Park, and the BeltLine.

Travel Tips

Safety
The source describes Atlanta as generally safe for visitors, especially in major tourist areas.

Safety recommendations from the source:
- Avoid walking alone late at night.
- Do not leave valuables visible in cars.
