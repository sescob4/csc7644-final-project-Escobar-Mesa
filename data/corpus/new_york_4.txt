New York Travel Guide: Must-Sees and Hidden Gems

Overview
New York City is known for iconic landmarks, Broadway, neighborhoods, parks, museums, rooftop views, shopping, food, and skyline photography. Most attractions in this guide are located in Manhattan, with additional experiences in Brooklyn and Staten Island.

Best Time To Visit New York

Spring and Fall
Spring and fall are considered some of the best times to visit New York.

Highlights:
- Comfortable sightseeing weather.
- Less extreme temperatures.
- Good walking conditions.

Winter
Winter in New York is cold and can be snowy, but it is often less crowded.

Highlights:
- Lower hotel prices.
- Holiday decorations in December.
- Christmas lights and seasonal activities.

Travel note from the source:
- January and February can offer strong travel deals.

Summer
Summer in New York is hot and humid.

Travel note from the source:
- Many locals leave the city on weekends during peak summer heat.

Free and Low-Cost Attractions

Central Park
Location:
- Between the Upper West Side and Upper East Side, Manhattan

Central Park is one of New York’s most famous green spaces and includes walking paths, fountains, skyline views, and recreational areas.

Highlights:
- Bethesda Fountain.
- Bethesda Terrace.
- Loeb Boathouse.
- Skyline views.
- Walking paths.
- Nature inside the city.

Travel note from the source:
- Levain Bakery cookies were recommended nearby.

Travel tip from the source:
- Visitors can plan routes through the park in advance using a map.

Times Square
Location:
- Midtown Manhattan

Times Square is known for massive digital billboards, bright lights, and entertainment.

Highlights:
- Nighttime atmosphere.
- Broadway Theater District.
- Busy pedestrian intersections.
- Iconic New York experience.

Travel note from the source:
- Times Square is especially recommended after dark.

Grand Central Terminal
Location:
- Midtown Manhattan

Grand Central Terminal is one of New York’s most famous transit hubs and architectural landmarks.

Highlights:
- Decorative ceilings.
- Historic architecture.
- Dining options.
- Famous movie location.

Travel note from the source:
- “Secrets of Grand Central” tours are available.

Staten Island Ferry
Location:
- Lower Manhattan to Staten Island

The Staten Island Ferry is a free ferry route that passes by the Statue of Liberty.

Highlights:
- Statue of Liberty views.
- Manhattan skyline views.
- Free attraction.

Travel information from the source:
- Ferries run approximately every 25 minutes.
- The route spans approximately 8.5 kilometers.

Travel note from the source:
- Riders can return immediately to Manhattan without exploring Staten Island.

Nearby Staten Island attractions mentioned:
- Zoo.
- Museums.

The High Line
Location:
- West Side of Manhattan

The High Line is an elevated public park built on a former railway line.

Highlights:
- Elevated walking paths.
- City views.
- Public art.
- Landscaped gardens.

Travel information from the source:
- Approximately 2.4 kilometers long.
- Free admission.

Travel note from the source:
- The High Line connects conveniently to Chelsea Market.
