New Orleans Attractions, Food, Music, and Travel Guide

Overview
New Orleans, Louisiana, is known for jazz music, nightlife, food, historic architecture, festivals, cocktails, museums, and cultural experiences. Popular areas include the French Quarter, Garden District, Frenchmen Street, Jackson Square, and the Mississippi River waterfront.

Top Tours and Experiences

Popular tours and activities include:
- Sightseeing bus tours through the French Quarter, Garden District, and Treme.
- Evening jazz cruises on the Mississippi River.
- Cajun and Creole cooking classes with meals included.
- Whitney Plantation tours focused on slavery history.
- Jazz brunch at Court of Two Sisters.
- Swamp tours outside the city.

Things To Do in New Orleans

Bourbon Street
Bourbon Street is the most famous nightlife street in New Orleans and is located in the French Quarter.

Highlights:
- Live music venues.
- Bars and nightlife.
- Souvenir shops.
- Oyster bars.
- Street performers.
- Historic French Quarter atmosphere.

Popular drinks include:
- Hurricane cocktail.
- Sazerac.
- Hand Grenade.
- Shark Attack.

Travel tips:
- Bourbon Street becomes crowded at night.
- Parallel streets are often quieter and less tourist-heavy.

Suggested time to visit:
- Daytime sightseeing: 1 to 2 hours.
- Nightlife: several hours.

Preservation Hall
Preservation Hall is one of the most famous jazz venues in New Orleans and features live acoustic jazz performances.

Highlights:
- Traditional jazz performances.
- Intimate music venue.
- Historic New Orleans music experience.

Travel tip:
- Tickets are required.

Jackson Square
Jackson Square is a historic public square located in the French Quarter near St. Louis Cathedral.

Highlights:
- Street musicians.
- Artists and vendors.
- Historic architecture.
- Local performers.
- Fountain and statues.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 2 hours.

New Orleans Museum of Art (NOMA)
NOMA is the oldest art museum in New Orleans and includes modern and contemporary art collections.

Highlights:
- International art collections.
- Rotating exhibitions.
- Contemporary art.
- Besthoff Sculpture Garden.

Travel information:
- Sculpture Garden admission is free.
- Closed Mondays.

Suggested time to visit:
- 2 to 4 hours.

French Market
The French Market has operated since 1791 and includes souvenirs, local food vendors, candy shops, and handmade goods.

Highlights:
- Souvenir shopping.
- Local food stands.
- Handmade jewelry and crafts.
- Pralines and local desserts.

Popular foods:
- Fried alligator.
- Fresh oysters.
- Local candy and pralines.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 3 hours.

St. Louis Cathedral
St. Louis Cathedral was originally built in 1727 and is one of the most recognizable landmarks in New Orleans.

Highlights:
- Historic church.
- Victorian architecture.
- Stained glass windows.
- Free admission.

Travel tips:
- Photography is allowed.
- Visitors should remain respectful because it is an active church.

Suggested time to visit:
- 30 minutes to 1 hour.

Brennan’s
Brennan’s is a famous New Orleans restaurant known for its pink exterior and the creation of Bananas Foster.

Popular items:
- Bananas Foster dessert.

Highlights:
- Historic restaurant.
- Tableside flambé preparation.
- French Quarter dining destination.

Storyville Museum
The Storyville Museum focuses on the history of the Storyville district and early New Orleans red-light culture.

Highlights:
- Historic exhibits.
- Brothel recreations.
- Early New Orleans social history.
- Educational displays and illustrations.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 2 hours.

Garden District
The Garden District is known for historic mansions, tree-lined streets, boutiques, and restaurants.

Highlights:
- Victorian-style homes.
- Historic architecture.
- Magazine Street shopping and cafes.
- Scenic walking areas.

Travel information:
- Accessible via the St. Charles Streetcar Line.
- Jazzy Pass options available for multiple rides.

Suggested time to visit:
- 2 to 4 hours.

Frenchmen Street
Frenchmen Street is one of the best live music destinations in New Orleans and is popular with locals.

Popular venues include:
- Blue Nile.
- Cafe Negril.
- Maison.
- The Spotted Cat.

Popular restaurants:
- The Three Muses.
- Dat Dog.

Highlights:
- Live jazz music.
- Local nightlife atmosphere.
- Restaurants and bars.
- Less tourist-heavy than Bourbon Street.

Suggested time to visit:
- Evening and nighttime.

Cafe du Monde
Cafe du Monde is one of the most iconic food destinations in New Orleans and is famous for beignets and coffee.

Popular items:
- Beignets.
- Frozen Cafe Au Lait.
- Chicory coffee.

Highlights:
- Open 24 hours.
- Historic New Orleans cafe.
- French Quarter staple.

Travel tips:
- Cash only.
- Takeout lines move faster than dine-in lines.

Suggested time to visit:
- 30 minutes to 1 hour.

Cafe Beignet
Cafe Beignet is a smaller alternative to Cafe du Monde and is located on Royal Street.

Highlights:
- Quieter breakfast option.
- Beignets and coffee.
- Smaller cafe atmosphere.

Pharmacy Museum
The New Orleans Pharmacy Museum focuses on historical medicine, pharmacy practices, and medical artifacts.

Highlights:
- Apothecary jars.
- Historic medical instruments.
- Eyeglass exhibit.
- Historic living quarters.

Travel information:
- Admission approximately $10 for adults.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 2 hours.

Faulkner House Books
Faulkner House Books is a small independent bookstore located in Pirates Alley near Jackson Square.

Highlights:
- Rare and local books.
- Historic bookstore.
- Literary atmosphere.

Travel tip:
- Limited occupancy due to small size.

New Orleans Jazz Museum
The Jazz Museum focuses on musicians, jazz history, instruments, and interactive exhibits.

Highlights:
- Fats Domino exhibit.
- Interactive music displays.
- Jazz history collections.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 2 hours.

Mardi Gras World
Mardi Gras World displays parade floats, costumes, and Mardi Gras history.

Highlights:
- Mardi Gras float exhibits.
- Guided tours.
- Interactive experiences.
- Photography opportunities.

Travel information:
- Tours run approximately every 30 minutes.
- Adult admission approximately $22.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 2 hours.

National WWII Museum
The National WWII Museum is one of the largest museums in New Orleans and contains interactive exhibits, aircraft, and historical displays.

Highlights:
- WWII aircraft and artifacts.
- Interactive exhibits.
- Military history collections.

Travel information:
- Located in the Warehouse District.
- Several hours recommended for full visit.

Suggested time to visit:
- 3 to 5 hours.

Cemetery Tours
New Orleans is known for above-ground cemeteries and historic mausoleums.

St. Louis Cemetery No. 1
Highlights:
- Historic above-ground tombs.
- Marie Laveau burial site.
- Nicolas Cage pyramid tomb.

Travel information:
- Guided tickets required for Cemetery No. 1.

Cemetery No. 3
Highlights:
- Historic cemetery photography.
- Above-ground mausoleums.

Guided Bus Tours
Guided tours often include:
- French Quarter.
- Frenchmen Street.
- Garden District.
- Cemetery No. 3.
- Treme neighborhood.

Highlights:
- Local storytelling.
- Architecture and history.
- Celebrity homes and landmarks.

Congo Square and Louis Armstrong Park
Congo Square is considered one of the birthplaces of jazz music.

Historical significance:
- Gathering place for enslaved Africans and Caribbean communities.
- Music, dance, and cultural traditions developed here beginning in the 1700s.

Highlights:
- Jazz history.
- Cultural significance.
- Louis Armstrong Park.

Mississippi River Jazz Cruise
Jazz cruises on paddlewheel boats offer live music and views of the Mississippi River.

Highlights:
- Live jazz performances.
- Riverfront views.
- Optional dinner packages.

Suggested time to visit:
- Evening activity.

Audubon Aquarium and Zoo
The Audubon Nature Institute includes both an aquarium and zoo.

Zoo highlights:
- Asian elephants.
- Giraffes.
- Tigers.
- White alligators.

Aquarium highlights:
- Shark touch tank.
- Mississippi River exhibit.

Additional attractions:
- Lazy river.
- Water slides.

Highlights:
- Family-friendly attraction.
- Wildlife and conservation exhibits.
- Interactive experiences.

Voodoo Museum
The Voodoo Museum focuses on voodoo culture and New Orleans spiritual history.

Highlights:
- Historic artifacts.
- Sculptures and exhibits.
- Marie Laveau history.

Travel information:
- Small museum.
- Adult admission approximately $10.

Swamp Tours
Swamp tours outside New Orleans provide opportunities to see local wildlife and wetlands.

Wildlife commonly seen:
- Alligators.
- Raccoons.
- Wild boar.

Recommended tours:
- Honey Island Swamp Tour.
- Jean Lafitte tours.

Travel information:
- Many tours include hotel pickup.

JAMNOLA
JAMNOLA is an immersive art and culture attraction celebrating New Orleans music, art, and culture.

Highlights:
- Interactive exhibits.
- Photo opportunities.
- Installations by local artists.
- Recycled and upcycled art materials.

Suggested time to visit:
- 1 to 2 hours.

Transportation in New Orleans

Hop-On Hop-Off Bus
Highlights:
- Transportation between major attractions.
- Guided narration.
- Good overview of the city.

Streetcars
The St. Charles Streetcar is one of the most iconic transportation experiences in New Orleans.

Highlights:
- Historic transportation system.
- Scenic route through the city.

Walking
Many major attractions in the French Quarter are walkable.

Travel tip:
- Wear comfortable shoes because visitors often walk extensively.

Bicycle Tours
Bike tours are popular for exploring neighborhoods and learning city layout.
