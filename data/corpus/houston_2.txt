Houston Travel Guide

Overview
Houston, Texas, is known for cultural diversity, museums, space exploration, Tex-Mex cuisine, barbecue, Viet-Cajun food, coffee shops, nightlife, and sprawling neighborhoods. Popular areas include Montrose, The Heights, EaDo, Downtown Houston, and the Museum District.

Houston Facts

Highlights:
- One of the most ethnically diverse major cities in the United States.
- More than 145 languages are spoken in Houston.
- Home to the largest livestock show and rodeo in the world.
- The Texas Medical Center is the largest medical complex in the world.
- Houston contains a six-mile underground pedestrian tunnel system downtown.

Travel Information:
- Houston is not considered highly walkable.
- A car is strongly recommended for most visitors.
- Winter and early spring are considered the best times to visit because temperatures are cooler and humidity is lower.

Top Things To Do in Houston

Space Center Houston
Space Center Houston is the official visitor center for NASA’s Johnson Space Center.

Highlights:
- Replica Space Shuttle Independence.
- International Space Station exhibits.
- Historic Mission Control tours.
- George W.S. Abbey Rocket Park.
- Saturn V rocket display.

Travel tips:
- Tram tours can sell out on busy days.
- Booking tours online in advance is recommended.
- Houston CityPASS may help reduce ticket costs.

Address:
1601 E NASA Pkwy, Houston, TX 77058

National Museum of Funeral History
The National Museum of Funeral History explores burial traditions, funeral customs, embalming history, and cultural approaches to death.

Highlights:
- Ancient and modern funeral practices.
- Presidential hearses.
- International burial traditions.
- Dia de los Muertos exhibits.
- Historical artifacts and displays.

Address:
415 Barren Springs Dr, Houston, TX 77090

Museum of Fine Arts, Houston
The Museum of Fine Arts, Houston is one of the largest art museums in the United States.

Highlights:
- Multiple museum buildings.
- Contemporary art.
- Decorative arts.
- International exhibitions.
- Frida Kahlo exhibits.
- Underground tunnels connecting buildings.

Travel tips:
- Parking rates increase after four hours.
- Exploring one building at a time is recommended.

Address:
1001 Bissonnet St, Houston, TX 77005

Meow Wolf Houston’s Radio Tave
Radio Tave is an immersive art installation and interactive storytelling experience.

Highlights:
- Interactive exhibits.
- Multi-dimensional themed environments.
- Art installations.
- Hidden passages and secret rooms.
- Cowboix Hevvven restaurant and bar.

Travel tips:
- Earlier entry times provide more exploration time.

Address:
2103 Lyons Ave Building 2, Houston, TX 77020

Houston Travel Tips

Best Time To Visit
Recommended travel months:
- November through March.

Reasons:
- Lower humidity.
- Cooler temperatures.
- More comfortable outdoor weather.

Getting Around
Transportation tips:
- A car is highly recommended.
- Houston is very spread out.
- Avoid toll roads in rental cars when possible.
- Parking costs vary by location.

Airports
Houston airports include:
- George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAH).
- William P. Hobby Airport (HOU).

Local Cuisine
Houston food culture includes:
- Tex-Mex.
- Viet-Cajun cuisine.
- Barbecue.
- West African food.
- Global fusion cuisine.

Popular local foods:
- Crawfish boils.
- Queso.
- Breakfast tacos.
- Brisket.
- Boudin kolaches.

Safety
General safety recommendations:
- Lock vehicles.
- Avoid leaving valuables in cars.
- Practice defensive driving because Houston traffic can be aggressive.
